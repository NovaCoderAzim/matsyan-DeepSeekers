About Our Project

Revolutionizing fishing with smart technology

DeepSeek is a web-based fishing route optimizer that transforms raw marine data into actionable insights. By using your current sea location, we pull real-time weather data through APIs like StormGlass and OpenWeatherMap. We then score surrounding zones based on wave height, wind, visibility, and temperature — and show you the best fishing areas on an interactive map. This helps fishermen make smarter, faster, and safer decisions.
